# Digital Business FEB UNPAD

### Bridging Business, Technology, and Data Analytics

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of the **Digital Business Study Program, Faculty of Economics and Business, Universitas Padjadjaran (UNPAD), Indonesia**.

As the first Digital Business study program established in Indonesia, we integrate business knowledge, digital technology, and data analytics to prepare future-ready graduates for the evolving digital economy.

### 🎯 Our Focus

- 💼 **Digital Business & Entrepreneurship** — Building innovative, technology-driven business models.
- 📊 **Data Analytics & Business Intelligence** — Transforming data into actionable insights.
- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** — Developing intelligent solutions for business challenges.
- 💳 **Financial Technology & Digital Finance** — Exploring technology-enabled financial innovation.
- 🚀 **Digital Product Development** — Turning ideas into valuable digital products and services.
- 🌐 **Digital Transformation** — Empowering organizations to thrive in the digital economy.

### 💻 Our GitHub Community

This organization is a collaborative space for students, lecturers, researchers, and industry partners to:

- Share academic projects and programming portfolios.
- Develop data analytics and machine learning applications.
- Build digital business prototypes and technology-based ventures.
- Explore research, experimentation, and open-source innovation.
- Collaborate on real-world business and technology challenges.

### 🤝 Industry & Global Collaboration

Our learning ecosystem connects academic knowledge with industry practice through company collaborations, internships, and professional competency development.

Students are also encouraged to pursue international experiences through student exchange programs and double-degree opportunities for eligible students in the International Undergraduate Program (IUP).

### 🌱 Learn. Build. Analyze. Innovate.

We believe the future of business belongs to those who can bridge **business strategy, technological innovation, and data-driven decision-making**.

Together, let's build solutions that create meaningful impact.
